# Sprint 1 Review

## Terminology

- **Sprint Review:** The penultimate event within the Sprint. The Scrum Team
  presents the results of its work to key stakeholders, discusses progress
  toward the Product Goal, and may adjust the Product Backlog.
- **Increment:** A usable result that provides value and meets the applicable
  Definition of Done.
- **Product Goal:** The future product state that guides planning.
- **Product Backlog:** The ordered, evolving list of work needed to improve the
  product.
